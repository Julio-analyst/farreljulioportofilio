FOOD PRICE MONITORING &EARLY WARNING SYSTEM
Developed an integrated food price monitoring and Early Warning System using PIHPS data for
nine strategic commodities. The system processes updated price data through validation,
cleaning, transformation, and structured storage before distributing it into two analytical panels.
The Food Price Monitoring panel presents price levels, movements, historical trends, and
commodity comparisons, while the Early Warning System applies statistical growth and volatility
indicators to calculate the Indicator of Price Anomalies and classify emerging price risks.
Data Source: PIHPS
Data Processing: Python, Pandas, NumPy
Statistical Methods: CQGR, CAGR, Rolling Volatility,
PCA, IPA
Visualization: Power BI, Looker Studio
Outputs: Food Price Monitoring Panel and EWS
Risk Panel
Integrated PIHPS-based price monitoring and decision-support system. 
Project overview
The Food Price Monitoring panel provides an
interactive view of price developments across
commodities and periods. It enables users to monitor
current price levels, daily and monthly movements,
historical trends, and unusual changes through
filters, charts, and summary indicators.
Monitoring Pangan
The Early Warning System aggregates daily
PIHPS data into monthly observations and
calculates CQGR, CAGR, rolling volatility,
volatility-adjusted growth, quarterly IPA, and
annual IPA. PCA-derived weights are applied to
generate a composite IPA score, which
classifies commodity conditions into Normal,
Alert, and Warning levels for early risk
monitoring.
